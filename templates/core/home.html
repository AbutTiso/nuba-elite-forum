About NEF

Together for Unity, Equality, and Progress

Nuba Elite Forum (NEF) is a professional and inclusive platform established to unite intellectuals, professionals, academics, students, community leaders, entrepreneurs, and change-makers from the Nuba Mountains and beyond.

At a time when the people of the Nuba Mountains and Sudan continue to experience complex humanitarian, developmental, and governance challenges, there is an urgent need for a credible forum where ideas, expertise, and experiences can be exchanged for the common good.
